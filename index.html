Think like the herd. Win Cow Coins.

How to Play

  1. A prompt appears, like “Best topping on pancakes”.
  2. Everyone thinks during the countdown (if you picked one).
  3. At “MOO!”, all players shout their answer out loud at once.
  4. The host taps every player who matched the majority (the herd) to give them a Cow Coin 🪙.
  5. Tap the big area to moove on to the next prompt.
  6. First to 20 Cow Coins wins — or the host can End Game any time.

Don't be a black sheep — er, cow. Think like everyone else!
